Questions

How early should we book?

Two weeks ahead is ideal. For weddings and peak dates, book a month in advance. We also take short-notice bookings when our calendar allows.

Can you decorate at my home or terrace?

Yes. We style homes, terraces, rooftops, banquet halls and offices. Our team visits or reviews photos of the space first.

Can we customise colours and the neon sign text?

Every setup is customised, including colours, names, numbers and the message on the sign.

Can we add a photographer to our booking?

Yes. Add photography to any decor booking, or book it on its own. You receive an edited gallery within 5 days.
